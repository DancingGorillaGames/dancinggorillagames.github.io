Tidy Belt

Coming soon to Google Play

Things come rolling down the conveyor belt. Tap to send each one into the basket of its colour, swipe away what doesn't belong, and watch a messy room become spotless.

Privacy policy

What's inside

  • Six cozy roomsPlant corner, kitchen, toy room, picnic, bakery and bathroom, each with three things to sort that take turns.
  • Quick to learnTap left, middle or right to sort, and swipe away the odd one out.
  • Streaks and starsKeep sorting without a mistake to build a streak, earn more coins and collect stars.
  • Hard and Extreme levelsEvery few levels the belt speeds up and mistakes cost hearts. Boosters help you through.
  • Endless modeOne long run against your own best score.
  • Make it yoursFind new belts, baskets and trucks in chests. Colour-blind patterns can be turned on in Settings.

Good to know

Tidy Belt is free to play. It shows ads between some levels and offers optional ads that give you a reward, and you can buy Remove Ads or a Starter Pack in the shop. Your progress is saved on your phone. See the privacy policy for what the game and its services collect.
