Tidy Belt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Tidy Belt (“the game”) is made by Dancing Gorilla Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to us at dancinggorillagames@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don’t ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any account.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your device.
  • The game uses a few trusted services to show ads, process purchases, understand how the game is played and fix crashes. They receive device information such as your advertising ID.
  • You can turn off personalised ads, reset your advertising ID, or ask us to delete data at any time.

Information saved on your device

The game keeps a save file on your phone with your level progress, coins, boosters, upgrades, skins, settings (sound, music, haptics, colour-blind patterns) and whether you bought Remove Ads or the Starter Pack. This file stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If your device backs up app data (for example Android Auto Backup), your save may be included in that backup under your own account.

Ads and your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game’s settings.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking). On iPhone the game asks for permission before using your IDFA.
  • Remove Ads. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads that appear between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay optional.

How we use information

We use information only to run the game, show ads, deliver purchases, understand and improve gameplay, and fix problems. We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t use it to identify you personally. Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and on our legitimate interest in running, improving and securing the game for analytics, remote configuration and crash reports.

Children

Tidy Belt is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 14 months, then deleted or anonymised. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies. You can ask us to delete data linked to your device at any time by writing to dancinggorillagames@gmail.com with the subject “Tidy Belt data deletion”. Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to object to or limit how it is used, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security and transfers

The services we use protect information with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit. They may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, under safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will let you know in the game.
